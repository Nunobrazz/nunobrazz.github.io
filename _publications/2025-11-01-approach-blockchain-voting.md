We explore a blockchain-based voting protocol tailored for low-electorate, weighted-voting environments. The blockchain ensures correctness and censorship resistance. Threshold Fully Homomorphic Encryption (TFHE) and signatures of knowledge ensure correctness, privacy and weak coercion resistance, assuming that a minimum number of voters are honest.
